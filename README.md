# CCTS — Customer Complaint Tracking System

## Project Overview

CCTS (Customer Complaint Tracking System) is a multi-channel complaint management platform designed to help businesses receive, track, manage, and resolve customer complaints efficiently.

The system allows customers to submit complaints through supported communication channels without unnecessary restrictions. It uses automation to capture complaint details, identify missing information, retain supporting evidence, and provide prompt responses. When human intervention is required, staff can take over the complaint with access to the customer's information and conversation history.

CCTS aims to improve the customer complaint experience, streamline internal workflows, and ensure that complaints are properly documented and handled from submission to resolution.

## Project Objectives

- Provide customers with simple and accessible ways to submit complaints.
- Automate complaint intake and reduce the need for manual data entry.
- Ensure customers receive timely confirmation and responses.
- Preserve complaint details, attachments, and conversation history.
- Help staff prioritize, investigate, and resolve complaints efficiently.
- Enable managers to oversee complaint handling and manage automation rules.

## Supported Channels

Phase 1 focuses on two customer complaint submission channels:

- **WhatsApp:** Customers can submit complaints through WhatsApp, including voice notes and supporting media.
- **Web Form:** Customers can submit complaints through a web-based form.

The initial language requirements are English and Nigerian Pidgin.

## User Roles

### 1. Customer

Customers can submit complaints, provide additional information when requested, attach supporting evidence, and receive confirmation and updates about their complaints.

### 2. Staff

Staff members can review assigned complaints, access customer and order information, view conversation history, correct automated decisions, update complaint statuses, add internal notes, document resolutions, and hand over complaints to other staff members.

### 3. Manager / Admin

Managers and administrators can oversee complaint handling, review cases requiring approval, correct mistakes, reassign complaints, and manage automation settings according to business rules.

## Core Features

- **Multi-Channel Complaint Intake:** Receive complaints through WhatsApp and web forms.
- **Automated Complaint Capture:** Convert customer submissions into structured complaint records.
- **Voice Note Processing:** Convert voice complaints into text for processing.
- **Evidence Management:** Retain photos and recordings alongside complaint records.
- **Missing Information Collection:** Request only the additional information needed to process a complaint.
- **Customer Notifications:** Provide timely confirmation and responses after complaint submission.
- **Complaint Tracking:** Maintain complaint details and status throughout the resolution process.
- **Staff Dashboard:** Give staff access to complaint records, customer details, order information, and conversation history.
- **Priority Management:** Make high-priority complaints easy to identify.
- **Complaint Resolution:** Support status updates, internal notes, resolution records, and staff handovers.
- **Administrative Controls:** Allow managers to oversee complaints, manage assignments, approve relevant actions, and configure automation rules.

## Project Scope

The initial release focuses on multi-channel complaint intake and the supporting workflows required to manage complaints effectively.

The frontend will provide interfaces for customers, staff, and managers, with each interface designed around the responsibilities and information needs of its intended users.

Future interface development will follow the approved user flows, wireframes, and UX requirements established during the design phase.

## Project Goal

The goal of CCTS is to make complaint handling more accessible, organized, transparent, and efficient by combining automated intake with effective human oversight.

The platform is intended to help customers report problems easily while giving staff and managers the information and tools they need to handle complaints through to resolution.
